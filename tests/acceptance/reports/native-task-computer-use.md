find_roots located its modal root (@r9, then @r10), but observe_ui refused it with
`Root is not owned by a running app`; cached search also failed capture with
window 0. This is a concrete **extension/native-dialog targeting limitation**,
not evidence that product quit failed or an excuse to force-kill the app.

same window. A second referenced Cmd-Q plus native AXPress Stop and Quit cleaned

confirmed quit after the Stop cases, not active/hidden/multi-session quit-matrix
qualification. Only after native quit was the owned supplier closed through its
private control; PID **75524** exit was verified. No process-name kill occurred.
